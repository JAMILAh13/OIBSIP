## Objective

The objective of this task was to research the importance of patch management, explain why unpatched systems are one of the largest attack surfaces in cybersecurity, and explore how organisations can implement an effective patch management strategy to reduce cyber risk.

## Learning Outcomes

Through completing this project, I learned:

- Why patch management is one of the most important cybersecurity processes.
- How vulnerabilities are discovered, tracked, and assigned CVE identifiers.
- The importance of testing and verifying software updates before deployment.
- How delayed patching can lead to ransomware attacks, data breaches, and financial losses.
- Why patch management should be treated as both a cybersecurity responsibility and a business priority.
